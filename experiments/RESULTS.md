# Results

Generated by `python experiments/run.py`. Every number is the mean over 5 random seeds unless marked otherwise. All data is synthetic: made-up names, so nothing can be known in advance. Raw numbers are in `results.json`.

## 1. Learning from small datasets

Two unrelated sets of 108 made-up facts ("A" then "B"). "Told once" means each fact is stated a single time. The comparison is the model's own network with episodic memory and System 2 switched off, trained at its full learning rate on every statement, which is how a conventional network would learn the same stream.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| full model, told once | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| network alone, told 1x | 6% | 8% | 0% |
| network alone, told 20x | 71% | 67% | 0% |

## 6. Every interaction becomes training data

A 300-step conversation with no training phase. The teacher states facts, asks questions, praises right answers, rebukes wrong ones and then gives the right one, and answers when the model says it does not know. Now and then a fact changes without the model being told. A turn "changed what is learned" if the hash of the cortex weights, memory traces, prohibitions, caution level and open questions differs after it. Pain levels and drive tensions are not in the hash, because they drift with time anyway.

## 7. Desire: what curiosity buys

Half the facts are told. Then the model asks 60 questions of its own and the teacher answers those that have an answer. With drives on, curiosity picks the question the model is least sure about. With drives off it picks at random.

|  | known after 0 questions | known after 20 questions | known after 40 questions | known after 60 questions | questions that taught something new |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| curious (drives on) | 50% | 63% | 75% | 86% | 39.0 |
| drives off (asks at random) | 50% | 56% | 61% | 67% | 18.8 |
